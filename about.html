Who We Are

SYH Aviationmechanics, students, and aviation professionals. We believe technology should improve safety, learning, and decision-making while keeping humans at the center of aviation.

Our Mission

To build trusted aviation tools through listening, learning, and collaborating with the aviation community.

Our Vision

To become a respected platform where aviation professionals can share ideas, solve problems, and help shape the future of aviation technology.

Our Principles

  • ✈️ Safety First
  • 🤝 Trust & Transparency
  • 🔒 Privacy Matters
  • 👨‍✈️ Human Judgment Comes First
  • 💡 Continuous Innovation
