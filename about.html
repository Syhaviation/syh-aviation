About SYH Aviation

Building technology that supports aviation professionals, never replaces them.

Who We Are

SYH Aviation is a community built for pilots, instructors, mechanics, students, and aviation professionals.

We believe technology should improve safety, learning, and decision-making while keeping humans at the center of aviation.

Our Mission

To build trusted aviation tools through listening, learning, and collaborating with the aviation community.

Our Vision

To become a respected platform where aviation professionals can share ideas, solve problems, and help shape the future of aviation technology.

What We're Building

✈️ Flight Schedule Pro

A modern scheduling platform designed to make flight planning and organization easier for aviation professionals.

🤝 Aviation Community

A growing community where pilots, mechanics, instructors, students, and aviation enthusiasts can connect, learn, and share ideas.

📚 Learning

Resources that encourage continuous learning and help people better understand the aviation industry.

💼 Opportunities

Connecting aviation professionals with future career and industry opportunities.

Our Principles

  • ✈️ Safety First
  • 🤝 Trust & Transparency
  • 🔒 Privacy Matters
  • 👨‍✈️ Human Judgment Comes First
  • 💡 Continuous Innovation

Our Commitment

We are committed to building aviation technology responsibly, listening to the people who use it, and creating tools that make aviation safer, smarter, and more connected.

Join the Journey

The future of aviation is built together. We welcome pilots, instructors, mechanics, students, developers, companies, and aviation enthusiasts who want to help make a difference.
